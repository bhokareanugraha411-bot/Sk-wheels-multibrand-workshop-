About SK Wheels Multibrand Workshop

A multibrand car workshop on Kuchi Road, near Balaji Mandir, Kavathe Mahankal.

SK Wheels handles service, denting and painting, alignment and balancing, washing and mechanical work, along with spare parts. The workshop is run by Mr. Shashikant Khot and Mr. Shahaji Khot.

Because everything is under one roof, you can bring your car in once and have several jobs looked at, whatever the brand.
